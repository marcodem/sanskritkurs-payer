# Lesson 37

## 37.1. The Adverb (⟪क्रियाविशेषणम्⟫)

In Sanskrit, adverbs are formed

1. by case forms of nouns and pronouns
2. with the help of special suffixes and endings

In the first case, the boundary between living cases and adverbs is fluid. This includes as a special case those adverbs that are fossilized, old case forms, while in declension other forms are used or the corresponding nouns are no longer used as nouns.

## 37.2. Adverbial Use of Case Forms

### 37.2.1. Accusative (⟪द्वितीया⟫)

::: grammar-box
The accusative (⟪द्वितीया⟫) can be used adverbially in response to the questions:

*   in what manner?
*   for how long?
*   whither?
*   etc.

The accusative can be used adverbially:

*   from substantives  
Examples:  
⟪कामम्⟫ "as desired, according to wish"  
⟪अर्थम्⟫ "because of"

*   from adjectives in the neuter  
Examples:  
⟪सुखम्⟫ "easily, happily"  
⟪नित्यम्⟫ "always"
⟪साधु⟫ "correctly, well"

*   from pronouns in the neuter  
Examples:  
⟪तद्⟫ "then, therefore"  
⟪यद्⟫ "when, that"
⟪एतद्⟫ "thus, here, now"
:::
### 37.2.2. Instrumental (⟪तृतीया⟫)

::: grammar-box
The instrumental (⟪तृतीया⟫) can be used adverbially in response to the questions:

*   by what means?
*   with what?
*   etc.

The instrumental can be used adverbially:

*   from substantives  
Examples:  
⟪अर्थेन⟫ "because of"  
⟪क्षणेन⟫ "at the moment, immediately" (from ⟪क्षण⟫ m. "moment")

*   from adjectives, often also in the plural form  
Examples:  
⟪दूरेण⟫ "far, from afar" (to ⟪दूर⟫ 3 "far, distant from")  
⟪उच्चैस्⟫ "high, loud" (to ⟪उच्च⟫ 3 "high, elevated, loud, shrill (of voice)")
:::
### 37.2.3. Dative (⟪चतुर्थी⟫)

::: grammar-box
Rarely is the dative (⟪चतुर्थी⟫) used adverbially in response to the questions:

*   for what purpose?
*   and similar.

Example:
:::
::: indent
⟪अर्थाय⟫ "for the purpose of, for the sake of ..."
:::
### 37.2.4. Ablative (⟪पञ्चमी⟫)

::: grammar-box
The ablative (⟪पञ्चमी⟫) can be used adverbially in response to the questions:

*   why?
*   on account of whom/what?
*   from where?
*   and similar.

The ablative can be used adverbially:

*   from substantives  
e.g. ⟪बलात्⟫ "violently, by force"  

*   from adjectives  
e.g. ⟪दूरात्⟫ "from afar"  

*   from pronouns  
Examples:  
⟪कस्मात्⟫ "why"
⟪अकस्मात्⟫ "unexpectedly"
:::
### 37.2.5. Genitive (⟪षष्ठी⟫)

::: grammar-box
Rarely is the genitive (⟪षष्ठी⟫) used adverbially in response to the question "after how long a time?"

e.g. ⟪चिरस्य⟫ "after a long time" (to ⟪चिर⟫ 3 "long (time)")
:::
### 37.2.6. Locative (⟪सप्तमी⟫)

::: grammar-box
The locative (⟪सप्तमी⟫) can be used adverbially in response to the questions:

*   where?
*   under what circumstances?
*   and similar.

Examples:
:::
::: indent
\-⟪अर्थे⟫ "because of"  
\-⟪कृते⟫ "for the sake of"  
⟪दूरे⟫ "far away"
:::
## 37.3. Adverbial Use of No Longer Common Case Forms

Examples:
::: indent
⟪जातु⟫ "at all" (originally "by birth, by nature", acc. sg. n. to ⟪जातु⟫ \<no longer commonly used as a noun\>)

⟪तूष्णीम्⟫ "silently" (acc. sg. to a no longer common ⟪तूष्णी⟫ f. "silence")

⟪एवम्⟫ "thus" (to an otherwise lost pronominal stem ⟪एव⟫ "one, single")
:::
## 37.4. Formation of Adverbs with Adverbial Suffixes

::: grammar-box
From the pronominal stems ⟪तद्⟫, ⟪इदम्⟫ or respectively ⟪अ⟫-, ⟪यद्⟫, ⟪किम्⟫ or respectively ⟪कु⟫, one can derive with the adverbial suffixes

*   \-⟪तस्⟫ (Ablative)
*   \-⟪त्र⟫ (Locative)
*   \-⟪था⟫ (Manner)
*   \-⟪थम्⟫ (Manner)
*   \-⟪दा⟫ (Time)

corresponding pronominal adverbs.
:::
::: grammar-box

| Suffix | Pronominal Stem | | | |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| | ⟪तद्⟫ | ⟪इदम्⟫ :br **⟪अ⟫-** | ⟪यद्⟫ | ⟪किम्⟫ :br **⟪कु⟫-** |
| **-⟪तस्⟫** :br(Ablative) | ⟪ततस्⟫ :brfrom there, on that account, therefore | ⟪इतस्⟫ :br ⟪अतस्⟫ :brfrom here, on this account | ⟪यतस्⟫ :brfrom where, from what | ⟪कुतस्⟫ :brfrom where?, why? |
| **-⟪त्र⟫** :br(Locative) | ⟪तत्र⟫ :brthere, in that place | ⟪अत्र⟫ :brhere | ⟪यत्र⟫ :brwhere | ⟪कुत्र⟫ :brwhere? |
| **-⟪था⟫** :br(Manner) | ⟪तथा⟫ :brthus, so | | ⟪यथा⟫ :brhow | |
| **-⟪थम्⟫** :br(Manner) | | | | ⟪कथम्⟫ :brhow? |

:::

## 37.5. Adverbial Compounds

### 37.5.1. Compounds with an Adverb or a Case Used Adverbially as the Final Member

Example: ⟪सुचिरम्⟫ "for a very long time"

### 37.5.2. Adverbially Used ⟪बहुव्रीहि⟫

Example: ⟪रक्षार्थम्⟫ "in the manner of someone whose purpose is protection, for the sake of protection". It can also be considered belonging to 5.1.: -⟪अर्थम्⟫ as an adverbial final member.

### 37.5.3. ⟪अव्ययीभाव⟫ Compounds

⟪अव्ययीभाव⟫ = "become unchangeable/indeclinable"

::: grammar-box
An ⟪अव्ययीभाव⟫ is an adverbial compound whose initial member is indeclinable (postposition, particle, etc.) and whose final member is a noun taking the ending of the Accusative Singular Neuter.

When resolving the compound, the initial member would govern the final member:

Examples:
:::
::: indent
⟪प्रत्यग्नि⟫ = ⟪अग्निं प्रति⟫ = "against the fire"

⟪यथाशक्ति⟫ = ⟪शक्तिमनतिक्रम्य⟫ = "to the best of one's ability"

⟪अभिमुखम्⟫ = ⟪मुखम् अभि⟫ = "forward, towards"
:::
Particularly important are ⟪अव्ययीभाव⟫ with ⟪यथा⟫ "like" as the initial member:

e.g. ⟪यथाकामम्⟫ = ⟪कामो यथास्ति⟫ = "as desired, according to wish, arbitrarily"

## 37.7. Indefinite Pronouns

::: grammar-box
Indefinite pronouns are formed by appending

*   \-⟪चिद्⟫
*   \-⟪चन⟫
*   ⟪अपि⟫

to the cases of the interrogative pronoun ⟪किम्⟫.

e.g.
:::
::: indent
⟪कश्चिद्⟫ = ⟪कश्चन⟫ = ⟪को ऽपि⟫ = "someone, anybody"  
⟪कस्यचिद्⟫ = someone's
:::
## 37.8. The Interrogative Sentence

::: grammar-box
"Who?, how?, what?, why? for what reason?  
He who does not ask remains ignorant."

⟪कः कथं किं केन कस्मात्कस्मै । यो न पृच्छेन् मूर्खस्तिष्ठेत् ॥⟫

Motto of Sesame Street, which serves as a good guideline for life and the study of Sanskrit
:::
See also [Lesson 4](lektion04.md).

One must distinguish between

*   Word questions (substitution questions)
*   Sentence questions

In word questions, one asks about an agent (⟪कर्तृ⟫), an object, an action, a circumstance, etc., e.g.

*   who is coming?
*   what is Rāma doing?
*   whom does he hear?
*   how does she speak Sanskrit?
*   where is the mother?

In sentence questions, the entire content of the sentence is queried, e.g.

*   is Rāma going to Ofterdingen today?
*   is it true that ... ?

### 37.8.1. Word Questions (Substitution Questions)

::: grammar-box
Schema:

Interrogative pronoun/interrogative adverb - sentence
:::
Examples:
::: indent
⟪को ग्रामं गच्छति⟫ =  ⟪केन ग्रामो गम्यते⟫ = "Who is going to the village?"

⟪किं बाला अधीयीरन्⟫ = "What should children learn?"

⟪क्व रामो वसति⟫ = Where does Rāma live?
:::

![lekt3704.jpg](/images/lekt3704.webp)
Fig.: ⟪किं बाला अधीयीरन्⟫

### 37.8.2. Yes/No Questions

::: grammar-box
Sometimes it is only clear from the context whether a sentence is a question.
:::
::: grammar-box
Schema I:

Verb - rest of the sentence
:::
Example:
::: indent
⟪गमिष्यति ब्राह्मणबालो गुरुम् ।⟫ "Will the Brāhmaṇa boy go to a master?"
:::
::: grammar-box
Schema II:

Question particle (usually at the beginning of the sentence) - sentence

The most important question particles are:

*   ⟪अपि⟫

*   ⟪किम्⟫ (often suggests a negative answer is expected)

*   ⟪कच्चिद्⟫


These particles correspond to the question mark.
:::
Example:
::: indent
⟪अपि सत्यम् ।⟫ "Is it also true?"

⟪किं शक्नोति ।⟫ "Can he, then?"

⟪कच्चित्क्षत्रियेण शत्रवो जिताः ।⟫ "Has the Kṣatriya perhaps defeated the enemies?"
:::
Note: ⟪किम्⟫ can mean not only "what?" but also as a question particle "why?, for what purpose?". Especially with the instrumental case (⟪तृतीया⟫):
::: indent
⟪किं क्रोधेन ।⟫ "For what purpose the anger? What is the use of the anger? What should be the purpose of the anger?"
:::

## 37.11. Review Exercise

Translate into Sanskrit using exclusively perfect tense verb forms:

Once, when an old man was going to another village, he became tired on the way. So he went to rest at the foot of a mango tree standing by the side. On this tree there were ripe fruits. The old man desired these. But he could not climb the tree and reach for the fruits. Fortunately, there were some monkeys on this tree that ate fruits. When he saw them, the old man rejoiced. What did he do? He took some stones, aimed at the monkeys and threw them. The enraged monkeys picked up some fruits and threw them on the old man. The old man joyfully took these and went to his desired place. Behold, the fate of the old man!
