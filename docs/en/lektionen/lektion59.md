## 59.5. Aorist (⟪लुङ्⟫) 6: siṣ-Aorist (P only)

::: grammar-box
**The siṣ-Aorist is formed in the P by all roots ending in -ā, -e, -o, -ai that are not restricted to the 1st, 2nd or 3rd Aorist, as well as by some other roots, including ⟪नम्⟫, ⟪यम्⟫, ⟪रम्⟫. In the Ā all these roots form the s-Aorist.**

**Formation:**

**Augment + strengthened root + s + i + ṣ + athematic secondary ending (like the iṣ-Aorist)**
:::

## 59.6. Aorist (⟪लुङ्⟫) 7: sa-Aorist (thematic s-Aorist)

::: grammar-box
**The sa-Aorist is formed by most ⟪अनिट्⟫-roots ending in -ś, -ṣ or -h with i, u or ṛ in the penultimate position. Optional ⟪अनिट्⟫-roots of this shape form either the sa-Aorist or the iṣ-Aorist.**

**Formation:**

**Augment + weak root + s + a + thematic secondary ending**

**The 1.sg.2.3.du.Ā are formed by the s-aorist, i.e., athematically.**
:::
