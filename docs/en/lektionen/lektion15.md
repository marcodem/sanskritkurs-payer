## 15.2. Determinative compounds = Tatpuruṣa m. = ⟪तत्पुरुष⟫

The relationship between entities designated by nouns (substantives and adjectives) can be expressed not only through a genitive construction but also through a Tatpuruṣa (⟪तत्पुरुष⟫). Similarly, attributive coordination of adjectives or appositional coordination of substantives can be expressed through a specific type of Tatpuruṣa, namely the so-called Karmadhāraya (m.) = ⟪कर्मधारय⟫.

⟪तत्पुरुषः⟫ =  ⟪तस्य पुरुषः⟫ "his servant", i.e., as a designation for this type of compound, an example of such compounds is used.
::: grammar-box

In determinative compounds (Tatpuruṣa), a noun (substantive or adjective) is specified in more detail by another noun or adverb. The word being specified usually forms the second member of the compound.

The relationship between the first member (determining element) and the second member (determined element) can be:

*   attributive or appositional: upon resolution of the compound, the first member forms a specification in the same case as the second member (i.e., outside of sentence context, in the nominative (⟪प्रथमा⟫); within a sentence, in the syntactically required case)
*   causal: upon resolution of the compound, the first member stands in a different case than the second member (i.e., not in the nominative - ⟪प्रथमा⟫)
*   adverbial: the first member is an indeclinable word

The case relationship of both components of a compound is independent of the case in which the compound stands: for the compound is, after all, a single declinable word:

e.g.

> Nom. sg. ⟪तत्पुरुषः⟫ = ⟪तस्य पुरुषः⟫  
> Acc. sg. ⟪तत्पुरुषम्⟫ = ⟪तस्य पुरुषम्⟫  
> Instr. sg. ⟪तत्पुरुषेण⟫ = ⟪तस्य पुरुषेण⟫  
> Gen. sg. ⟪तत्पुरुषस्य⟫ = ⟪तस्य पुरुषस्य⟫  
> Nom. pl. ⟪तत्पुरुषाः⟫ = ⟪तस्य पुरुषाः⟫  
> etc.

The gender of a Tatpuruṣa is, with few exceptions, that of its second component.

:::
## 15.3. Determinative compounds with attributive / appositional first component = Karmadhāraya m. = ⟪कर्मधारय⟫
::: grammar-box

When resolving a Karmadhāraya, both components of the compound stand in the same case.

:::
e.g.

⟪गुणवत्पुत्रः⟫ = ⟪गुणवान्पुत्रः⟫ = "a son with good qualities"  
Acc. sg. ⟪गुणवत्पुत्रम्⟫  
Nom pl. ⟪गुणवत्पुत्राः पुण्यवत्क्षत्रिया⟫ = ⟪पुण्यवती क्षत्रिया⟫ = "a Kṣatriya woman of merit"  

⟪साधुजनाः⟫ = ⟪साधवो जनाः⟫ = "good people"  

⟪इष्टदेवता⟫ = ⟪इष्टा देवता⟫ = "the desired deity = the deity to which one has a special relationship of devotion and refuge"

::: media
![](/images/lekt1501.webp)
Fig.: ⟪लक्ष्मी⟫
(Image source: [Details](/en/licenses#lekt1501))
:::

::: grammar-box

The following special rule applies to the sequence of components in a Karmadhāraya:

*   if a Karmadhāraya expresses a COMPARISON, then that with which the comparison is made stands in the second component of the compound:

⟪नरसिंहः⟫ = ⟪सिंह इव नरः⟫ = "a man like a lion"  
⟪पुरुषव्याघ्रः⟫ = ⟪व्याघ्र इव पुरुषः⟫ = "a man like a tiger"

*   however, if a Karmadhāraya expresses an apposition (further specification of the second member by a noun), then the specifying element stands in the first member, as also required by the general rule for the order of members in a Tatpuruṣa:

⟪नरसिंहः⟫ can also be resolved: ⟪नर एव सिंहः⟫ = "A lion, who (in reality) is a man."

Such appositional Karmadhārayas are resolved in the indigenous commentaries, as in the example above, by means of ⟪एव⟫.

## 15.4. Determinative compounds with a first member that stands in a non-attributive/appositional case relationship to the second member = Tatpuruṣa in the narrower sense

::: grammar-box

Tatpuruṣa combinations are possible for connections of nouns (nouns and adjectives), where the first member - according to the rules of syntax - can stand in any case. As expected, the first member most frequently represents a genitive (⟪षष्ठी⟫), since this is indeed the case for expressing the relationship between nouns.

:::
e.g.

⟪क्षत्रियपुत्रः⟫ = ⟪क्षत्रियस्य पुत्रः⟫ = "the son of a Kṣatriya", "a young Kṣatriya", "a member of the group of Kṣatriyas"  
Acc. sg. ⟪क्षत्रियपुत्रम्⟫  
Gen. sg. ⟪क्षत्रियपुत्रस्य⟫  
etc.  

⟪गुरुभावः⟫ =  ⟪गुरोर्भावः⟫  = "the nature of a teacher"

⟪धनलोभः⟫ = ⟪धनस्य लोभः⟫ = "desire for wealth, avarice"

⟪लोकगतिः⟫ =  ⟪लोकस्य गतिः⟫ = "the course of the world, the behavior of people"

Almost every genitive relationship can be replaced by a Tatpuruṣa. The few exceptions see e.g. in Kale, A higher Sanskrit grammar § 211; there you will also find the corresponding passages in Pāṇini.

However, the first member of a Tatpuruṣa can in principle represent any case. Nevertheless, not all syntactically possible case relationships can be replaced by a Tatpuruṣa. The corresponding rules can be found in Kale, A higher Sanskrit grammar §§ 203–217 or Pāṇini 2,1,22–2,2,22 in case of doubt.

::: grammar-box

The first member can stand in the singular, dual, or plural when resolving the compound. Which possibility is present must be inferred from meaning and context.

:::
Examples:

The first member represents the accusative (⟪द्वितीया⟫): e.g. in certain PPPs with verbs of motion (Pāṇini 2,1,24):

⟪ग्रामगतः⟫ = ⟪ग्रामं गतः⟫ = "one who has gone into the village"
⟪नरकपतिता⟫ = ⟪नरकं पतिता⟫ = "one who has fallen into a hell"

The first member represents the instrumental (⟪तृतीया⟫): e.g. frequently the agent (⟪कर्तृ⟫) of nominal formations with kṛt suffixes (e.g. PPPs):

⟪देवकृतम्⟫ = ⟪देवेन⟫ / ⟪देवैः कृतम्⟫ = "made by a god / made by gods"
Also resolvable as: ⟪देवस्य⟫ / ⟪देवानां कृतम्⟫ = "deed/action of a god / by gods; divine deed, deeds of the gods"

⟪बुद्धरक्षिता⟫ = ⟪बुद्धेन रक्षिता⟫ = "the one who was protected by the Buddha" (a proper name)

## 15.5. Decomposition of compounds (except Dvandvas)

::: grammar-box
Although compounds of arbitrary length can be formed in Sanskrit and are also very frequently formed (compounds with 10 to 30 members are no rarity!), nevertheless, with the exception of Dvandvas, all compounds must be decomposed progressively and hierarchically into two parts each:

:::
::: media
![](/images/lekt1502.jpg)
Fig.: ⟪समासविच्छेदः⟫
(Image source: [Details](/en/licenses#lekt1502))
:::
etc. until one reaches the individual word stems.

e.g.

⟪गुणवत्पुत्रकृतपुण्यम्⟫

::: indent
1st stage (main caesura): ⟪गुणवत्पुत्रकृतं ॥१॥ पुण्यम्⟫

2nd stage (first secondary caesura): ⟪गुणवत्पुत्रेण ॥२॥ कृतं ॥१॥ पुण्यम्⟫

3rd stage (second secondary caesura): ⟪गुणवता॥३॥ पुत्रेण ॥२॥ कृतं ॥१॥ पुण्यम्⟫

= "the meritorious deed (merit) that my virtuous son has performed"
:::
Various types of compounds can be mixed here, e.g. foreground element: Bahuvrīhi (⟪बहुव्रीहि⟫) - background element: Tatpuruṣa, etc.

e.g.

⟪ब्राह्मणक्षत्रियवैश्यधर्मः⟫

::: indent
1st stage: ⟪ब्राह्मणक्षत्रियवैश्यानां धर्मः⟫ (foreground element: Itaretaradvandva)

2nd stage: ⟪ब्राह्मणानां क्षत्रियाणां वैश्यानां च धर्मः⟫

= "the dharma of Brahmans, Kṣatriyas and Vaiśyas"
:::
Very often there are various possibilities for resolving a compound. Whether the correct or at least the best one is, can only be decided from the context and content of the text. Sometimes such a decision is not possible. Often two resolution possibilities are presumably intended by the author. Then both resolution possibilities must be rendered in the translation (connected with "and", "or" "respectively" and similar).

e.g.

⟪पुण्यवत्पुत्रकृतम्⟫

::: indent
1st stage: either - or

1. ⟪पुण्यवत्पुत्रेण कृतम्⟫
2. ⟪पुण्यवत्पुत्रस्य कृतम्⟫
3. ⟪पुण्यवत् पुत्रकृतम्⟫

2nd stage: accordingly

1. ⟪पुण्यवता पुत्रेण कृतम्⟫ = "that which was done by my meritorious son"
2. ⟪पुण्यवतः पुत्रस्य कृतम्⟫ = "the deed of my meritorious son"
3. 1. ⟪पुण्यवत् पुत्रेण कृतम्⟫ = "the meritorious, done by my son"
2. ⟪पुण्यवत् पुत्रस्य कृतम्⟫ = "the meritorious deed of my son"
:::
## 15.6. The Form of the First Member in Compounds (⟪समास⟫ m.)
::: grammar-box

In all types of compounds, the first member is generally the unchanged stem. Two-stem nouns stand in the weak stem. Feminine adjectives, which further specify a following member in the compound, generally stand in the masculine stem:

e.g.

⟪पुण्यवत्क्षत्रिया⟫ = ⟪पुण्यवती क्षत्रिया⟫ = "a Kṣatriya woman who possesses merit"  
⟪गुणवत्पुत्रः⟫ = ⟪गुणवान् पुत्रः⟫ = "a son with good qualities"

:::
## 15.7. Classification of ⟪तत्पुरुष⟫

1.  ⟪प्रथमातत्पुरुषः⟫ : First member stands in ⟪विग्रहवाक्य⟫ in the Nominative (⟪प्रथमा⟫)
2.  ⟪द्वितीयातत्पुरुषः⟫ : First member stands in ⟪विग्रहवाक्य⟫ in the Accusative (⟪द्वितीया⟫)
3.  ⟪तृतीयातत्पुरुषः⟫ : First member stands in ⟪विग्रहवाक्य⟫ in the Instrumental (⟪तृतीया⟫)
4.  ⟪चतुर्थीतत्पुरुषः⟫ : First member stands in ⟪विग्रहवाक्य⟫ in the Dative (⟪चतुर्थी⟫)
5.  ⟪पञ्चमीतत्पुरुषः⟫ : First member stands in ⟪विग्रहवाक्य⟫ in the Ablative (⟪पञ्चमी⟫)
6.  ⟪षष्ठीतत्पुरुषः⟫ : First member stands in ⟪विग्रहवाक्य⟫ in the Genitive (⟪षष्ठी⟫)
7.  ⟪सप्तमीतत्पुरुषः⟫ : First member stands in ⟪विग्रहवाक्य⟫ in the Locative (⟪सप्तमी⟫)

*   ⟪कर्मधारयः⟫ : appositional Tatpuruṣa, among others:
*   ⟪उपमानपूर्वपदकर्मधारयः⟫ : ⟪विग्रहवाक्य⟫ with ⟪इव⟫ after the first member
*   ⟪उपमानोत्तरपदकर्मधारयः⟫ : ⟪विग्रहवाक्य⟫ with ⟪इव⟫ after the second member
*   ⟪रूपकसमासः⟫ : ⟪विग्रहवाक्य⟫ with ⟪एव⟫
*   ⟪द्विगुसमासः⟫ : Numeral in the first member
*   ⟪नञ्तत्पुरुषः⟫ (⟪निषेधतत्पुरुषः⟫) : with negation a-, an- in the first member
*   ⟪गतिसमासः⟫ : with preposition in the first member
*   ⟪प्रथमातत्पुरुषः⟫ etc. (see above)

### 15.7.1. Classification of ⟪कर्मधारय⟫

1. ⟪विशेषणपूर्वपदकर्मधारयः⟫ : The first member is an adjective (⟪विशेषण⟫)
2. ⟪विशेषणोभयपदकर्मधारयः⟫ : both members are adjectives; this category also includes adjectives expressing a sequence of time: "first bathed, then anointed"
3. ⟪उपमानपूर्वपदकर्मधारयः⟫ (= ⟪उपमासमासः⟫): comparison in the first member, compared quality in the second member: e.g., "beautiful like a lotus"
4. ⟪उपमानोत्तरपदकर्मधारयः⟫ (= ⟪उपमितसमासः⟫): comparison in the second member
5. ⟪रूपकसमासः⟫ : ⟪विग्रहवाक्य⟫ with ⟪एव⟫ after the first member (metaphor)
6. ⟪संभावनपूर्वपदकर्मधारयः⟫ : both members refer to the same person or thing, e.g., ⟪कालिदासकविः⟫ = ⟪कालिदास इति कविः⟫ = "the poet Kālidāsa"
7. ⟪कर्मधारयः⟫ with ⟪कु⟫ / ⟪कद्⟫ as the first member: "bad..."
8. ⟪कर्मधारयः⟫ with ⟪किम्⟫ as the first member: censure
9. PPP + negated PPP: "partially", e.g., ⟪कृताकृतम्⟫ = "partially done"
10. ⟪द्विगुसमासः⟫ : numeral in the first member
